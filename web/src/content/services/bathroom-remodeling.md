Bathrooms are small rooms with a lot going on: water, ventilation, tile, fixtures. Jack builds them to look good on day one and still hold up years from now.

## What a bathroom project can include

- Tub, shower and tile work
- Vanities, counters and storage
- Flooring, lighting and fixtures
- Accessibility updates like grab bars and walk-in showers

## How it works

After a walkthrough, you get a written estimate and a clear plan for how long the bathroom will be out of use.
