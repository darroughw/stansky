A kitchen remodel touches almost every trade in the house, so it pays to have one builder who understands every phase. Jack plans the job with you, keeps the schedule moving, and is on site doing the work.

## What a kitchen project can include

- Cabinets, countertops and backsplash
- Flooring and trim
- Appliance and lighting updates
- Opening up or reworking the layout

## How it works

We start with a walkthrough of your kitchen to talk through what you want and what the space allows. From there you get a written estimate, a start date, and one point of contact from the first day to the final walkthrough.
