Outdoor projects are where Jack's carpentry background shows most. Whether it's a new deck, a porch that's past saving, or a screened room for bug-free evenings, every board is set by hand.

## What an outdoor project can include

- New decks and rooftop decks
- Porch rebuilds and new stairs
- Railings that meet code
- Screened porches

## How it works

Jack looks at the structure first. If an old deck or porch can be repaired, he'll tell you. If it needs to come down, you'll know why before you see a number.
