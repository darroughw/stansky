An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is a small, self-contained home on the same lot as your house. That could be a backyard cottage, a converted garage, or an apartment over a garage. Families use them for aging parents, grown kids, guests or rental income.

## What an ADU project involves

- Checking what your lot and local zoning allow
- Design and permits
- Foundation, framing and finishes
- Utility connections

## How it works

ADUs take more planning than a remodel. Jack starts by walking the property with you and talking through what you want the space to do.
